# Creative Website UI Profile

## Objective

Design distinctive public-facing websites where brand expression,
storytelling, composition, imagery, typography, and motion may be primary
requirements.

## Direction

- Establish a clear visual concept before implementation.
- Let the content and brand determine composition and hierarchy.
- Permit expressive layouts, intentional asymmetry, and varied pacing.
- Use typography, imagery, spacing, and motion to support the concept.
- Avoid defaulting to dashboard structure or a generic component showcase.
- Preserve accessibility, responsive behavior, performance, and semantic tokens.
- Do not introduce novelty that has no relationship to the content or brand.

## Skill selection

1. Load every skill listed in `required-skills`.
2. Load `frontend-design` when it is installed and the task needs art direction
   or expressive composition.
3. Load `emil-design-eng` when it is installed and the task involves animation,
   transitions, gestures, or interaction feel.
4. Load `impeccable` when it is installed and the task calls for critique,
   auditing, polishing, or reducing generic output.
5. If an optional skill is explicitly required by the spec but unavailable,
   stop and report the blocker.

## Review

Verify the approved visual direction, hierarchy, motion, responsive
composition, accessibility, performance, and consistency with the brand.
